## Phase rules

- Train on all 14 calendar days. The usual Wednesday full-rest rule is temporarily overridden only for this phase.
- Wednesday (2026-11-25 and 2026-12-02) is a low-stress active-recovery training day, not a normal lifting day: 15–20 minutes easy cardio, comfortable mobility, and easy technique work only. No hard sets or failure work.
- All regular lifting sessions use **2 working sets per exercise**. Keep approximately 3 reps in reserve during week 1 and 2–3 reps in reserve during week 2. Stop or reduce load if technique deteriorates, pain appears, or recovery is poor.
- Warm-ups are separate from the two working sets. For the first compound lift, use 5 minutes easy cardio, then 2–3 ramp-up sets (light × 10, moderate × 6, then an optional near-working warm-up × 3–5). For later exercises, use one light preparation set where needed. Do not count warm-ups as working sets.
- Use double progression conservatively. Add reps within the stated range before adding load. Do not increase load during this block merely because one set reaches the top of the range.
- The user explicitly sets Farmer's carries at **15 kg in each hand**, walking to and fro inside the gym, every training day. Use 2 rounds on normal lifting days; on Wednesday active-recovery days, use 1 easy round only if it remains genuinely easy.
- Seated knee raises: 50 bodyweight reps on every day except Wednesday.
- Side-lying leg raises (side-lying hip abduction): daily, including Wednesdays/rest days; latest confirmed performance is 10 kg per leg x 20 reps per leg on 2026-11-23. Keep effort controlled during active recovery.
- Leg press machine setting: 5. Calf raise machine setting: 5, same as leg press. Shoulder press machine setting: 5.
- Keep the regular weekly split in its original order. Do not shift a missed session to another day.
