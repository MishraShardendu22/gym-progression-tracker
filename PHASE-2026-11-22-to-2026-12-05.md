## Working sets and progression

The user normally performs working sets to failure. Select the heaviest practical load that permits at least 10 clean reps, then continue beyond 10 until failure. Ten is a minimum reference, not a cap.

- If failure occurs above 10 reps, assess the smallest practical load increase next session.
- If failure occurs below 10 reps, hold or reduce the load.
- At 10 reps to failure, hold unless the other sets/performance trend justifies an increase.
- Record each set individually with actual load and reps. Track weight PRs and rep PRs separately.
- Do not prescribe RIR by default. For RDL, stop at technical failure before form degrades; no forced reps.
- Normal lifting days retain two working sets per exercise for the duration of this temporary phase.
- Wednesday is active recovery only, no hard lifting sets or failure work.
