Place main/heaviest lifts early while fresh after the user's warm-up.

- If failure occurs above 10 reps, assess the smallest practical load increase next session.
- If failure occurs below 10 reps, hold or reduce the load.
- At 10 reps to failure, hold unless the other sets/performance trend justifies an increase.
- Record each set individually with actual load and reps. Track weight PRs and rep PRs separately.
